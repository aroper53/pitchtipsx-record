How we bet

  • Decimal odds on everything.
  • Best UK price, quoted with the bookmaker and the time it was checked.
  • Every result is logged. Wins, losses and voids. No deleted losers.
  • 1u = £5.
  • Staking: flat stakes on most markets. Player shots, player shots on target and team corners use a model-based quarter-Kelly stake of 0.5–2u, and there's no bet under a 3% edge.
  • One Telegram-exclusive pick a day. It goes to Telegram first and is logged on the record after kickoff.
  • Fun accas are just for fun. They're posted with no stake and not tracked. Bet builders with a real priced reason are staked and logged on the record like any other pick.
  • Closing-line value (CLV) is tracked to check whether we beat the closing price.
  • No profit promises. Betting carries risk. Only stake what you can afford to lose.
